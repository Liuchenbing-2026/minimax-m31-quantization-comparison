04 · 221G合理性与验收是两回事04 · Plausible size and verified correctness are different

410G → 221G

客户报告，单位及统计范围尚未核实。假设同模型、同单位、全量产物且其他字节不变：Customer-reported figures; units and scope remain unverified. Assuming the same model, units, complete artifact and unchanged other bytes:

E + O = 410
E / 2 + O = 221
E = 378, O = 32

这只是条件反推，与专家降半存储相容，不能证明没有缺片或量化正确。This conditional inference is consistent with halved expert storage, but does not prove completeness or correct quantization.

可以直接复用的验收方法Acceptance methods we can reuse

  1. 按层、rank、全局专家编号核对所有 gate/up/down 投影。Check every gate/up/down projection by layer, rank and global expert ID.
  2. 量化描述 ↔ safetensors 索引 ↔ 全部分片头/布局三层交叉核验。Cross-check quantization descriptions, safetensors indexes, and all shard headers/layouts.
  3. 核查 INT4 packing、scale 与旋转信息；物理 I8/I32 不等于逻辑位宽。Verify INT4 packing, scales and rotation metadata; physical I8/I32 dtype is not the logical bit width.
  4. 固定 A2/A3 软件栈，分别验证加载、实际 kernel、同口径精度及稳定性。Fix the A2/A3 software stack and separately validate loading, actual kernels, matched accuracy and stability.
DSV4.1 的 MTP 叶规约、DSpark 草稿浮点保护属于模型专属契约，不能直接复制到 MiniMax。metadata.verified_tags 也不是硬件验证证据。DSV4.1 MTP leaf normalization and floating-point DSpark draft protection are model-specific contracts, not rules to copy blindly to MiniMax. metadata.verified_tags is not hardware-validation evidence.

05 · 可直接向客户说明05 · Suggested customer response

与我们此前 V4.1 方案相比，您的混合位宽策略及专家量化核心参数基本一致，主要差异在于平滑算法、模块分组及模型专属导出处理。我们采用注意力 W8A8、路由专家 W4A8、共享专家 W8A8 三段显式配置；您采用专家 W4A8 加其余符合条件线性层 W8A8 的两段配置。当前 norm-linear 平滑不能直接视为与我们此前的专家 up-down 平滑等价。221G规模初步具有合理性，最终需结合全专家覆盖、导出完整性、目标环境兼容性和同口径精度评测确认。Your mixed-precision strategy and core expert quantization parameters broadly align with our earlier V4.1 approach. The main differences are smoothing algorithms, module grouping and model-specific export handling. We explicitly configured attention W8A8, routed experts W4A8 and shared experts W8A8 in three groups; your recipe uses expert W4A8 plus W8A8 for other eligible linear layers in two groups. The current norm-linear smoothing should not be assumed equivalent to our expert up-down smoothing. The reported 221G size is plausible, but full expert coverage, export integrity, target-environment compatibility and matched accuracy evaluation are still required.
